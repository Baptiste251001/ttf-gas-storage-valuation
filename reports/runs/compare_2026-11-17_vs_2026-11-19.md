## What changed and why

**Data.** The scraper was run twice on 2026-11-20 (11:22 and 12:10, `full` mode): 358 contracts, 0 errors, no new
or revised rows in the second pass. EEX publishes settlements in the evening, so the latest available session is
**2026-11-19**. The 2026-11-17 curve rebuilt from the hub is identical to the hand-typed EEX table used so far.

**Curve.** Between 17 and 19 November the whole storage-year strip fell by 0.8 to 1.3 EUR/MWh (-1.7 % on average).
Winter months fell more than summer months (-1.19 vs -0.90 EUR/MWh on average), so the seasonal backwardation
deepened from -7.43 to -7.71 EUR/MWh. The curve is still strictly decreasing: **intrinsic value stays at zero**.

**Historical-dynamics value: -0.205 MEUR (4.895 to 4.690, -4.2 %).** Both runs use the same seeds, so the change is
mostly the response to the curve, not Monte Carlo noise. A first-order attribution with the monthly deltas of the
market case on 2026-11-17 (notebook 05, 5,000 paths):

| | contribution |
|---|---:|
| summer months (Apr-Sep, net buyer: deltas -67 to +23 GWh) | +170 kEUR |
| winter months (Oct-Mar, net seller: deltas -1 to +111 GWh) | -356 kEUR |
| **total predicted by deltas** | **-186 kEUR** |
| observed | -205 kEUR |

The deltas explain about 90 % of the change; the remainder is within the standard errors of the deltas plus
second-order and two-day time effects. The level effect alone (value proportional to the curve level) would give only
-82 kEUR: the storage loses because it is, on average, a buyer of summer gas and a seller of winter gas, and winter
fell more than summer.

**Risk-neutral values: unchanged within error.** One-factor Q: LSMC -0.137 (-0.128 before), dual upper bound
0.146 MEUR (0.162 before); two-factor Q: 0.051 (0.081 before), both within about one standard error. The
risk-neutral range stays **0 to about 0.15-0.3 MEUR**: the headline conclusion does not depend on the curve date.
